TMP 2026

About the Conference

The 16th conference in the series “Typology of Morphosyntactic Parameters”, TMP 2026, will be held in Moscow on October 21–23, 2026.

The conference will be held in person in the conference hall of the Institute of Linguistics, Russian Academy of Sciences (1 Bolshoy Kislovsky Lane, Building 1; the hall is on the second floor). The possibility of remote participation will be announced later.

The conference is organized jointly by the Pushkin State Russian Language Institute, the Institute of Linguistics of the Russian Academy of Sciences, and Lomonosov Moscow State University.

Important Dates

  • July 31, 2026: abstract submission deadline
  • September 13, 2026: notification
  • October 21–23, 2026: conference

Call for Papers

Paper proposals are invited on the interaction of morphological and syntactic mechanisms, interfaces between syntax, prosody and information structure, and the interplay of general, type-specific and language-specific morphological and syntactic features through detailed analysis of the world’s languages. Submissions will undergo double-blind review.

  • abstracts must be between 1 and 2 pages, excluding references;
  • 12-point font, single spacing, and 2.5 cm margins;
  • abstracts must be anonymous and contain no references to grants or other funding;
  • each author may submit one single-authored and one co-authored abstract;
  • the conference languages are English and Russian.

Abstracts must be sent in both .doc(x) and .pdf formats to tmp.2018.moscow@gmail.com. The email should include each speaker’s full name, affiliation, email address, and contact telephone number.
